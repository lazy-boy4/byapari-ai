# Byapari AI — Product Context

## Register

**product** — dashboard and BI tool where design serves merchant tasks, not brand spectacle.

## Users

- **Primary:** Bangladeshi small/medium merchants (Facebook shop owners, Daraz sellers, neighborhood retailers) who export sales CSVs but lack analytics tooling.
- **Secondary:** Growing retailers checking dashboards weekly for promotions, restocking, and seasonal planning.
- **Tertiary:** Hackathon judges and evaluators who need a working demo, docs, and architecture clarity.

## Product Purpose

Convert a single sales spreadsheet into actionable business intelligence in under two minutes: KPIs, health score, AI recommendations (Bengali/English), forecasts, and pricing suggestions. Zero signup, zero database, zero integration work.

## Brand Personality

- **Confident, not flashy:** Professional tool for real shop owners, not a SaaS landing page.
- **Local-first:** Bengali and English as equals; currency in ৳; Bangladeshi seasonal context (Eid, bKash).
- **Transparent:** Data quality reports, connection status, explained health scores.
- **Paper editorial:** Print-inspired clarity from DESIGN.md; restrained ink and violet accent.

## Anti-References

- Dark glass-morphism BI dashboards (blue glow, gradient heroes)
- Generic SaaS gradient cards and side-stripe nav accents
- English-only analytics with machine-translated Bengali bolted on
- Hero-metric templates (giant number, tiny label, gradient accent)
- Crypto/fintech neon-on-black aesthetics

## Strategic Design Principles

1. **Action over charts:** Every metric pairs with "what to do next."
2. **Restrained color:** Paper tokens; violet secondary for primary actions only.
3. **Readable in daylight:** Light paper surfaces for shop-counter and morning review contexts.
4. **Familiar product patterns:** Sidebar nav, KPI grid, upload zone, status pills.
5. **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA, visible focus, Bengali glyph support in Roboto/Montserrat stack.

## Scene (Shape Brief — Confirmed)

Rafiq reviews weekly sales at a bright shop counter on phone or laptop, morning light, focused not flashy. Color strategy: **Restrained**. Fidelity: **production-ready, whole surface**.
